The QueensLink Ridership Study makes the case for full reactivation and integration of the LIRR Rockaway Beach Branch into NYC's subway system. Using the FTA's Simplified Trips-On-Project Software (STOPS), we modeled twelve service scenarios across three tiers, comparing corridor and system-level ridership impacts against the MTA's 2018 Sketch Assessment and 2023 20-Year Needs Assessment projections.
 
Co-authored with Zhexuan Tang. Published March 2026.
