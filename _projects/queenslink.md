The QueensLink Ridership Study, co-authored with Franklin Tang and published March 2026. makes the case for full reactivation and integration of the LIRR Rockaway Beach Branch into NYC's subway system. Using the FTA's Simplified Trips-On-Project Software (STOPS), the study models twelve service scenarios across three tiers, comparing corridor and system-level ridership impacts against the MTA's 2018 Sketch Assessment and 2023 20-Year Needs Assessment projections.

My contribution focused on scenario design, report writing, research-to-recommendation translation, and the development of the public-facing narrative for Queenslink.The study's findings were also incorporated into the QueensLink Initial Business Case, prepared by Steer, a global consultancy group.
