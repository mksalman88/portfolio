Lunch was $11.11 

11:11 pm, make a wish / Hold still, you say / My eyelash, plucked from my cheek, now on your fingertips / Make a wish / I exhale, sending it tumbling, spiraling / Like me / spiraling into you

Or maybe / since spiraling implies a sameness of an object throughout its motion / I am unwinding / unfurling / becoming undone

I re member it vividly: / the time your lips touched mine in a dark room and I felt / unmistakably bare

*some might say there are far worse crimes*, posits Hanif Abdurraqib / *but I believe failing the imagination of others might be the crime from which all other crimes are born*
