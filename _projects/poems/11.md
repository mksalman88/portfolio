Lunch was $11.11 

11:11 pm, make a wish / Hold still, you say / My eyelash, plucked from my cheek, now on your fingertips / Make a wish / I exhale, sending it tumbling, spiraling / Like me / spiraling into you

Or maybe / since spiraling implies a sameness of an object throughout its motion / I am unwinding / unfurling / becoming undone

I re member it vividly / The time your lips touched mine in a dark room and I felt / unmistakably bare / *Suspended and extended for what felt like a glorious hour* / *The hour I've dreamed of living again ever since*

*the final grains of it kissed* / *the mountain of sand at the bottom of the hourglass*
